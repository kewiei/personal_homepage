Tianmu Zhao

I am a data scientist interested in data science for urban computing and simulation.

I explore how data, models, and computational experiments can help us understand complex urban systems and support better decisions.

My interests span the full arc from observing cities through data to testing possible futures through simulation.

Research interests

My current interests include:

  • Urban computing — spatial, mobility, and infrastructure data.
  • Traffic simulation — computational environments and physical counterfactuals.
  • Safety evaluation — scalable evaluation of traffic scenes and emergency-vehicle interactions.
  • Data science — interpretable evidence for complex, real-world systems.
